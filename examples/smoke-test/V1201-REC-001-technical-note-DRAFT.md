## 4. TECHNICAL BASIS

### 4.1 Damage Mechanism

**Identified or probable damage mechanism:**
Two credible damage mechanisms are associated with this finding:

(a) General aqueous corrosion (CO2/H2S): The primary mechanism producing the
local metal loss (LTA) at CML SC2-06 is aqueous corrosion driven by CO2 and H2S
in the presence of free water at the lower shell zone. This is an active mechanism
in wet sour service on carbon steel. The 6 o'clock position at 2,400 mm from BTL
is consistent with the liquid accumulation zone where aqueous-phase corrosion is
most active.

(b) Hydrogen-induced cracking (HIC): The possible UT velocity anomaly at SC2-06
raises the question of HIC in the base metal. HIC is highly credible in wet H2S
service on carbon steel (see DMR V1201-DMR-001, DRAFT). HIC is NOT a metal loss
mechanism -- it produces internal laminar cracking parallel to the plate surface,
which can be indicated by a velocity anomaly on UT A-scan. HIC superimposed on
general corrosion would be an adverse combination requiring both a local metal
loss FFS assessment (Part 5) and an HIC/SOHIC assessment (Part 7).

**API 571 classification:**
(a) Aqueous corrosion / CO2 corrosion / H2S aqueous corrosion (API 571, wet H2S
damage mechanisms section, general/uniform corrosion section -- refer to current
edition for specific classification).
(b) Hydrogen-induced cracking (HIC) (API 571, wet H2S damage -- HIC section).

## 5. RISK CONSIDERATION

**Safety risk:**  [x] High   [ ] Medium   [ ] Low   [ ] Negligible

**Basis for safety risk assessment:**
V-1201 contains toxic sour gas (H2S). Any pressure boundary failure has the
potential for personnel fatality from H2S exposure. The dominant damage mechanism
(HIC/SOHIC in wet H2S service) can progress to through-wall cracking without
visible surface indication. The current inspection program (UT A-scan) is
poorly effective for detecting HIC/SOHIC. This combination of HIGH consequence
and INADEQUATE detection capability drives a HIGH safety risk.

**Environmental risk:**  [x] High   [ ] Medium   [ ] Low   [ ] Negligible

**Basis:**
Sour gas release would constitute a significant environmental incident
requiring regulatory notification. H2S is acutely toxic at low concentrations
and is subject to air emission regulatory limits.

**Production / reliability risk:**  [x] High   [ ] Medium   [ ] Low   [ ] Negligible

**Basis:**
V-1201 is the inlet separator for CDU Train A. A forced outage for unplanned
repair would shut down the entire CDU Train A processing stream.

**Failure mode if left unaddressed:**
General corrosion: progressive thinning leading to MAWP reduction and
potential leak or rupture if thickness falls below t_req.
HIC (if confirmed): laminar cracking potentially propagating to through-wall
failure or blister rupture; may progress without visible surface indication.
SOHIC (if confirmed): through-wall cracking at weld HAZ; can be rapid once
initiated.

**Probability of failure without action:**  [x] High   [ ] Medium   [ ] Low
(Provisional -- driven by wet H2S service, unconfirmed PWHT, and inadequate
current NDE for HIC/SOHIC. Probability to be reassessed after TOFD/PAUT results.)

**Consequence of failure:**
Toxic sour gas release; personnel safety hazard; environmental release; CDU shutdown.

**Time sensitivity:**
HIGH. TOFD/PAUT NDE should be conducted at the earliest opportunity (before

   component of this recommendation may be revised.
2. Operating conditions (pressure 2,000 kPa(g), temperature 50 deg C) are
   representative nominal values. Actual operating envelope confirmed from
   DCS records may affect assessment outcomes.
3. The vessel has not been subject to any recent process upsets, changes in
   service, or mechanical events beyond those documented in the inspection report.
   This assumption shall be confirmed with operations.
4. No weld seam runs through the SC2-06 LTA zone. If a weld is present, the
   Part 5 Level 1 structural discontinuity criterion will be more stringent and
   SOHIC at the weld HAZ becomes a more immediate concern.
5. PWHT was NOT applied at fabrication. This is a CONSERVATIVE assumption used
   in assessing SSC and SOHIC susceptibility. If PWHT is confirmed from the
   fabrication data report, susceptibility ratings may be revised downward.
