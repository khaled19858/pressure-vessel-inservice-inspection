> **Template usage notes (delete before issue):**
> - All blank fields must be completed or marked N/A with a written reason.
> - "ALERT" status in Section 8.2 means t_act approaches but has not yet reached t_min; engineer judgment and accelerated monitoring required.
> - "REJECT" status means t_act ≤ t_min; vessel shall not be returned to service until repaired, rerated, or accepted by an FFS assessment.
> - Corrosion rates in Section 8.3 shall be calculated using actual inspection interval between the previous and current readings; do not assume a nominal value without basis.
> - Section 14 (FFS) is not a substitute for a formal FFS assessment report. Where an FFS assessment is required, it shall be performed, documented, and attached.
> - Inspection intervals in Section 9.3 must satisfy API 510 maximum interval requirements and shall not be extended without RBI or engineering justification reviewed by the AI.
> - This template does not replace the Authorized Inspector's independent judgment.
